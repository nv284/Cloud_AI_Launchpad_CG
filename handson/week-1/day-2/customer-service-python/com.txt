1- kubectl get service customer-service

Then inspect the Service configuration:
-- kubectl describe service customer-service

Check the Deployment:
--- kubectl get deployment customer-service

Check the Pods::
--- kubectl get pods

And verify the endpoints:
---kubectl get endpoints customer-service

After correcting the YAML, they could apply it with:
--- kubectl apply -f k8s/customer-service-service.yaml

Then verify again:
--- kubectl describe service customer-service
--- kubectl get endpoints customer-service
